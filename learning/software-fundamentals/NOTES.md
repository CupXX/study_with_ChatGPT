# Teaching Notes — Software Fundamentals

- Language: Chinese by default.
- Focus on mechanisms and architecture rather than code syntax.
- Avoid turning lessons into code-reading or code-editing exercises.
- Prefer concrete system diagrams, data-flow walkthroughs, comparisons, and decision questions.
- Use real project situations as examples when they clarify a concept.
- Revisit earlier concepts during later lessons so understanding compounds.
- The learner should make meaningful architecture/product decisions during the final What's Dinner assessment, with ChatGPT providing guidance and correction.
- Do not assume fluency just because a concept sounded familiar; check understanding through explanation and transfer to a new scenario.
